## Summary

State the proposed change and the feedback or decision requested.

## Background and context

Describe the current situation, affected stakeholders, constraints, and why this proposal is being considered now.

## Problem and goals

State the problem to solve, intended outcomes, and what is explicitly out of scope (non-goals).

## Assumptions, dependencies, and access

List assumptions, external systems or teams, required permissions and access, and who owns each dependency. Verify critical access and availability at the start of the work; mark anything unverified with an owner and next step.

## Proposal

Explain what would change, why this approach addresses the problem, and how it is expected to work. Summarize the recommendation and a brief implementation outline; keep detailed task tracking in the project's existing tracker.

## Alternatives

Compare plausible options, including those rejected, and explain the trade-offs and evidence behind the recommendation.

## Risks and open questions

List risks and open questions with owners. Describe available evidence and what remains uncertain.

## Rollout and validation

Describe how the proposal will be validated, including experiments and acceptance criteria where useful. Outline implementation sequence, rollout, rollback, and immediate next steps. Track detailed tasks in the project's existing tracker rather than duplicating them here.
