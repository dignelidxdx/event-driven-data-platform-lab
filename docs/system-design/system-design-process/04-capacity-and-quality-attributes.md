# Capacity and Quality Attributes

Translate the workload and product expectations into quality-attribute scenarios. State the stimulus, operating conditions, expected system response, and measurable response target for concerns such as latency, availability, throughput, durability, security, and recoverability.

Estimate request and event rates, storage growth, and peak-to-average ratios when they influence design choices. Capture assumptions and sensitivity: show which estimates would materially change the architecture.

Define reliability measures at a level appropriate to the system. SLI and SLO targets describe observed service behavior and objectives; an SLA is an external commitment when one exists. RPO and RTO describe recovery objectives. Track operational measures such as MTTR when relevant. Use a [capacity estimate](../artifacts-and-templates/capacity-estimate.md) and [reliability review](../artifacts-and-templates/reliability-review.md) to make reasoning reviewable.
