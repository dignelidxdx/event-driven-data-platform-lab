# Requirements and Workload

Turn stakeholder needs into functional behaviors, measurable quality goals, constraints, and a workload model. Describe typical and peak traffic, read/write mix, payload sizes, growth expectations, and important usage patterns when that information is available.

Record uncertainty explicitly and identify which assumptions need stakeholder confirmation or measurement. Workload estimates should be transparent enough to revise; avoid false precision.

Use a [System Design Document](../artifacts-and-templates/system-design-document.md) to connect requirements to the evolving design. The Delivery Framework's [Requirements](../delivery-framework/01-requirements.md) and [API, protocols, and endpoints](../delivery-framework/03-api-protocols-and-endpoints.md) stages provide implementation-facing detail.
