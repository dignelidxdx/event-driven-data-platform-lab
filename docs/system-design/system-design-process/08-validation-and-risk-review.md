# Validation and Risk Review

Check that the proposal addresses the requirements and that its most important assumptions have evidence or a plan for validation. Review failure modes, security threats, capacity limits, operational readiness, migration concerns, and unresolved decisions.

Use prototypes, contract checks, load tests, or failure experiments where they can distinguish between design options. A [threat model](../artifacts-and-templates/threat-model.md), [reliability review](../artifacts-and-templates/reliability-review.md), and [design review checklist](../artifacts-and-templates/design-review-checklist.md) can structure the review.

Record risks with impact, likelihood, mitigation, owner, and validation status. Update the System Design Document, diagrams, contracts, and ADRs when evidence changes the design.
