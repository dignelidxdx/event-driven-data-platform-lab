# Problem and Scope

Agree on the problem before choosing a solution. Identify the stakeholders, users, affected workflows, business outcome, system boundary, and what is explicitly out of scope.

Capture the current situation and why it needs to change. Separate known facts from assumptions and questions. Define how stakeholders will recognize a useful outcome.

Create a concise [requirements brief](../artifacts-and-templates/requirements-brief.md). Use the Delivery Framework's [Requirements](../delivery-framework/01-requirements.md) stage to develop functional and non-functional requirements.
